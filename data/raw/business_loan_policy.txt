BUSINESS LOAN QUALIFICATION POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Eligibility Requirements
To qualify for a business loan, applicants must meet the following criteria:
- The business must have been in operation for a minimum of 12 months.
- The business must generate a minimum of $5,000 in monthly revenue.
- The business owner must have a personal credit score of 650 or higher.
- The business must be located within the United States.

2. Loan Terms and Amounts
- We offer loans ranging from $10,000 to $250,000.
- The repayment terms can be chosen from 12, 24, or 36 months.
- Interest rates start at 7.9% APR, depending on the applicant's creditworthiness.

3. Required Documentation
Applicants must provide the following documents during the final underwriting process:
- 6 months of recent business bank statements.
- The most recent business tax return.
- Government-issued ID of the primary business owner.

4. Excluded Industries
We do not provide funding to businesses in the following industries:
- Adult entertainment
- Cannabis dispensaries
- Real estate investment or speculation
- Non-profit organizations
